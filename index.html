Color Schemes

Five color tone categories.

Color Schemes refer to the systematic way of combining and applying colors
in design to create visual harmony, convey emotions and express a specific style.

In simple terms, a color scheme is the way colors are paired together
to help designers choose combinations that look balanced,
pleasing and thematically consistent.

Warm Tone

Give people warmth, vitality and enthusiasm

Cool Tone

Brings a feeling of peace, freshness, and serenity.

Neutral Tone

Elegant, natural, and effortlessly versatile.

Spring Palette

Bright, cheerful, and full of energy.

Summer Palette

Soft, refreshing, and light-toned.
